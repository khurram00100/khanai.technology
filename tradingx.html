AI trading intelligence

TradingX

An AI agent that ingests real-time market data and generates directional predictions for currencies, gold, and Bitcoin using machine-learning models and automated data pipelines.

Decision support

Market data into directional signals.

TradingX is an informational decision-support tool. It is not financial advice.

Core function

What TradingX does

Continuously ingests live market data, processes it through trained ML models, and outputs directional predictions such as up, down, or hold.

Real-time data pipeline
Coverage

Supported assets

Major forex pairs including USD, EUR, GBP, and JPY; gold (XAU/USD); Bitcoin (BTC/USD); and expansion through additional data-source integration.

Performance

Approximately 75% accuracy

Approximately 75% directional accuracy under standard market conditions. This refers to directional correctness, not price-level precision. Results vary by volatility.

Architecture

How it works

Connects to OHLCV market data APIs, preprocesses and normalizes data, runs trained classifiers, and returns a signal with a confidence score.

Technology

Built with

Python, Scikit-Learn, TensorFlow, Pandas, NumPy, LSTM, Random Forest, and Docker.
