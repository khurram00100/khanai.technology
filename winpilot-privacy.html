Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information the product may handle and the choices available to users.

Last updated: October 16, 2026 Effective date: October 16, 2026 Governing law: India Contact: winpilotai@gmail.com

Information handled

WinPilot AI may handle the following information when you use the relevant features:

  • Email address
  • Account name
  • Subscription plan information
  • Login timestamps
  • Chat messages and conversation history
  • Messages sent to a selected AI provider
  • Razorpay payment reference IDs
  • Application settings and user data stored locally
  • Optional cloud-synchronized chat data

Storage and AI providers

Chat history is stored locally in encrypted format. If optional cloud synchronization is enabled, chat data may be stored using Supabase over HTTPS.

Messages may be sent to the selected AI provider, which may include Google Gemini, OpenAI ChatGPT, or Anthropic Claude. Those providers apply their own privacy policies.

Voice, payments, and permissions

Voice recognition may be processed locally using offline models such as Vosk. Audio is not stored permanently by WinPilot AI.

Razorpay processes payments. WinPilot AI does not store full card numbers, CVV codes, or bank credentials. The app may request microphone, file-system, and internet access for documented features.

Your choices

Use the controls available in WinPilot AI to manage stored information. Contact winpilotai@gmail.com for support or deletion questions.

Changes

Material updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
