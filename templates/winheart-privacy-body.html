Local-first diagnostics

WinHeart AI is designed to run diagnostics locally on your Windows device. System health readings, repair history, notes, and settings are stored locally unless you explicitly enable a connected feature.

Information we may receive

Depending on the feature you use, the app may process your email or licence details for account and purchase support, system diagnostics you request, and messages you enter for assistance. We collect only what is needed to provide the requested feature or support.

Online assistance and payments

Online AI assistance or weather features are optional and can be disabled in settings. Payments may be handled by Cashfree; payment credentials are processed by the payment provider and are not stored by WinHeart AI.

Control and deletion

You can review local data controls in WinHeart AI, export available records, turn off optional online features, or request account and purchase support by emailing winpilotai@gmail.com.

Updates

We may update this policy as WinHeart AI evolves. The effective date on this page will change when material updates are made.
